About AspectLens

Full-stack aspect-based sentiment analysis system designed for university NLP research and real-world review mining.

End-to-End NLP Pipeline Architecture

The system implements a modular six-stage pipeline directly aligning with core Natural Language Processing curriculum units:

Unit 1 · Text Normalization & Sanitization
1. Preprocessing, PII Stripping & Offset Preservation

Cleans HTML, expands common contractions, and strips personally identifiable information (emails, phone numbers, URLs). Crucially maintains exact character offset mappings to the display text so frontend UI highlights can mark spans cleanly without text drift.

Unit 2 · Part-of-Speech Tagging & Chunking
2. Aspect Candidate Extraction

Provides three switchable extractors: (1) NLTK Regexp chunker with grammar NP: {<DT>?<JJ>*<NN.*>+}, (2) spaCy noun chunk parser, and (3) Syntactic dependency rules (copular predicates, adjectival modifiers). Filters out pronouns and generic nouns (e.g. thing, product, item, bit, stuff).

Unit 3 · Distributional Semantics & Taxonomies
3. Aspect Canonical Grouping

Unifies heterogeneous candidate variants (e.g. cost, value, price point) into 11 locked-in canonical categories using a prioritized cascade: (1) Domain Seed Lexicon (wins conflicts), (2) WordNet primary sense hypernyms/synonyms, and (3) all-MiniLM-L6-v2 centroid cosine similarity with Agglomerative Clustering.

Unit 4 · Syntactic & Dependency Parsing
4. Clause-Scoped Opinion Association

Associates evaluative words with aspects using syntactic dependency arcs first, with a fallback window of ≤ 5 tokens. Splits sentences at contrast conjunctions (but, however, although, though, yet) and guarantees opinions never link across contrast boundaries. Explicitly captures negation (not good, never worked) and intensifiers (too high, very fast).

Unit 5 · Sentiment Lexicons & Pretrained Transformers
5. Sentiment Scoring Interface

Behind a unified Scorer Protocol, supports: (1) Domain-adapted VADER with phrase rules, (2) Twitter RoBERTa sequence classification applied directly to the aspect's clause, and (3) Fine-tuned ABSA cross-encoder stub. Rule 6 enforcement: if no opinion word is found, strictly returns neutral with low confidence (0.30) without guessing.

Unit 6 · Information Extraction & Aggregation
6. Storage, Trends & Side-by-Side Analytics

Persists aspect mentions in SQLite with foreign key integrity. Computes mention volume alongside positive/negative percentages, chronological monthly trajectories, top representative snippets, and competitive delta analysis.

System Limitations & Ethical Disclosures
  • Review Verification: AspectLens processes and summarizes text provided by users or imported datasets. It does not authenticate whether an online review is genuine, incentivized, or synthetic.
  • Domain Sensitivity: Models evaluated on electronics (laptops/phones) may experience performance shifts when applied to other domains (e.g. restaurants or healthcare).
  • Statistical Caution: Low-frequency aspect categories (e.g. < 5 mentions) must be interpreted with caution. AspectLens always presents raw mention counts alongside percentages to prevent deceptive ratios.
  • Privacy First: No reviewer names, emails, phone numbers, or account IDs are stored in the database.
